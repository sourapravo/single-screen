01

Going to Movies

Cinema-going was never simply about watching a film. The journey to the theatre, the architecture of the hall, the crowd, the ticket and the machinery of projection were all part of the experience.

This prototype explores how these different elements can become part of a single research narrative. ¹

02

The Hall

The cinema hall can be understood not simply as a container for the screen, but as an assemblage of architecture, technology, people and urban movement.

The room, its entrances, seats, projection equipment and spectators form a connected environment rather than a collection of separate objects.

THE BUILT ENVIRONMENT

A hall is never only a room.

Architecture, machinery, bodies and movement meet here.

03

Technology

Projectors, sound systems, screens and electrical systems transformed the experience of exhibition over time.

The machine was never isolated from the space around it; it operated through workers, infrastructures, spectators and architectural arrangements.

04

Cinema and the City

Cinema halls were embedded within the changing geography of the city. Their locations, entrances, streets and neighbourhoods shaped the experience of moviegoing.

This section will eventually contain photographs, archival documents, maps and fieldwork material.
